Gold set labelling

Rules (read once before starting)
  • Label blind. Do not open data/labels.jsonl until you have exported.
  • Theme is the main complaint: what went wrong, not the tone. A specific complaint beats a general one ("worst app, OTP not coming" is login_otp).
  • Several complaints: the main one is what they spend the most words on; on a tie, the first. Put a clearly separate second one under "Second complaint".
  • generic_negative only when there is no usable detail. not_a_complaint for positive or neutral text. other for a specific complaint that fits nothing.
  • Owner is who would have to act. It is pre-filled from the theme; change it only when the review points elsewhere (a no-show marked by a driver who never called is ops; one marked by the system after a missed check-in is app; a complaint about the penalty is policy).
  • Safety: a risk to a person (unsafe or drunk driving, harassment, stranded at night, SOS or emergency helpdesk failing), or the reviewer says plainly that the service is unsafe. Lateness alone is not safety.
  • Blames an update: the reviewer mentions an update, new version, new feature or new UI.
  • Conventions for cases the themes do not name:
    • No way to give feedback or rate a trip, a feedback box that does not work, or a rating screen that only accepts high stars: support_unreachable with owner app.
    • "No option to call the driver": trip_details. A request for chat or messaging: feature_request.
    • Problems with the call itself (no audio, call drops, shows busy): other.
    • "App not working" with nothing else: generic_negative. "Not opening", "crashes", "blank screen": app_wont_open. "Keeps loading", "hangs": slow_performance.
  • Keys: → or Enter next, ← previous. Your work is saved in this browser as you go.
